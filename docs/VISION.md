# Evidence that helps us build better agents

ACP Lab helps people find out whether a change improves useful work. Turn a
question into a bounded experiment, observe what agents actually do, check the
result independently and use the evidence to decide what to improve.

For example, compare how two configurations handle a correction during work.
Capture what each received and delivered, inspect the actual result, and retain
the conditions that could explain the difference. A confident answer is something
to evaluate, not proof of success.

## Make comparisons honest and useful

The Lab connects independently configured agents through ACP. Scenarios can study
collaboration, execution, context and communication without requiring a particular
engine or fleet manager. Participants, retained state, opportunities, timing and
evaluators are experimental conditions, not hidden defaults.

Protocol fixtures establish particular connection behavior. Model trials expose
behavior under their stated conditions. Real people judge whether an experience
helped them. Reports should make these differences clear and let someone inspect
the evidence behind a conclusion without reading every event.

The goal is better decisions with less repetition and rescue. Prefer a meaningful
question and a check that can catch plausible failure over another impressive
score. Repeated trials should make limits and uncertainty clearer, not turn them
into broad claims.

The [local agreement](contracts.md) defines experiment integrity. The
[execution reference](execution-reference.md) describes scenarios, commands and
receipts; [Constellation agreements](https://github.com/microsoft/amplifier-constellation/blob/main/contracts/README.md)
own the shared agent and interaction promises being investigated.
